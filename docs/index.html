MoonPhase

An adaptive training coach for the MoonBoard.

What it is

The MoonBoard is a standard climbing wall with hundreds of set problems. MoonPhase runs your training session on it. After each problem you climb, it picks the next one for you — adjusted to how hard the last one felt and to the hold types you have already worked that session.

Why it exists

Climbers without a coach tend to train by feel. You pick the problems that look good, push hard on the ones your ego likes, and repeat your favourite style week after week. Over months that lopsided load — too many crimps, not enough rest on a tired day — is how finger, elbow and shoulder injuries start.

MoonPhase takes the choosing away. Every session builds up from easy problems, steps up only when you report one felt easy, and backs off when one felt hard or you failed it. It also spreads the work across hold types, so you are not doing four crimp problems in a row. You still train hard; you just stop picking the session that hurts you.

How to use it

  1. Sign up and set your board. Choose your hardest grade, your MoonBoard set and the wall angle.
  2. Start a session. MoonPhase shows you a problem on the board, with its holds marked.
  3. Climb it, then log the result. Tap Sent or Failed, then rate the effort from 1 (easy) to 10 (maximal). Not attempting it? Tap Skip.
  4. Get the next problem. It appears straight away — easier or harder based on your rating, and often a different hold type.
  5. End the session when you are done. It is saved. Open Past sessions any time to see every problem, rating and result.

Best used one-handed on a phone at the wall. A full session currently needs the 2016 or 2024 MoonBoard set. Your sessions are private to you.
